Innovate with AI — Student Competition

Develop and pitch an innovative solution leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cloud Technology.

Submission Deadline: September 26, 2025

Live Demos: November 8, 2025 at Odette School of Business

Submit Project Who is Eligible?

Who is Eligible?

Teams must consist of 2–4 undergraduate, graduate certificate, master’s, or Ph.D. students currently enrolled in a full-time or part-time program.
Recent graduates completing their degrees in 2024 are also eligible.
Teams must be able to attend the in-person DevFest on November 8, 2025, at the Odette School of Business, Windsor.

Rules

Projects should be new or substantially new for this competition.
Clearly disclose models, datasets, and APIs used.
Follow ethical, safety, and legal guidelines; no disallowed content.
Submissions must include: ≤3-minute video, repo/demo link, and 1-pager overview.

Prizes

Cash prizes will be awarded. Exact amounts will be announced soon with sponsors.

Timeline

September 26, 2025 — Submission deadline
October 2025 — Finalist notifications
November 8, 2025 — Live demos at DevFest Windsor

FAQ

Do we have to use Google tech?

No. You may use any responsible AI tooling. Google Cloud is welcome but not required.

Can non-students join?

At least one team member must meet the student or 2024 graduate eligibility.

Remote teams?

Submissions are online, but finalists must demo live in Windsor at DevFest.
