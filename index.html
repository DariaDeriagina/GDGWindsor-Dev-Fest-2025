What Is DevFest?

CONNECT LEARN GROW

DevFest Windsor is a community-led tech conference organized by Google Developer Groups that brings together developers, designers, students, and entrepreneurs. It features expert-led talks and hands-on workshops focused on the latest in artificial intelligence, cloud and web technologies, and startup innovation. A dedicated high-school track offers practical labs—like Arduino robotics—to inspire the next generation of tech talent.

Tracks & Themes

Choose the path that fits your skills and interests — four distinct tracks await.

AI & Machine Learning

Explore the latest in generative AI, LLMs and computer vision. Learn how to train models, address ethics & bias, and apply AI responsibly in real projects.

Cloud & Web Technologies

Get hands‑on with scalable cloud architectures, serverless patterns and modern web frameworks. Discover best practices for security, performance and CI/CD.

Startup & Innovation

Hear from founders and VCs about ideation, funding and go‑to‑market strategies. Join pitch sessions and design sprints to bring your own innovation to life.

Full‑Stack Development

Deep dives into modern web stacks: React/Angular front‑ends, Node/.NET back‑ends, CI/CD.

Cybersecurity

Get hands‑on with scalable cloud architectures, serverless patterns and modern web frameworks. Discover best practices for security, performance and CI/CD.

High‑School Track

We invite you to enroll your children in a one-day workshop specifically designed for high school students. Students will work in groups to assemble a car kit, program its controls, and develop collision avoidance logic using proximity sensors.
