High-School Track

A one-day, hands-on robotics & coding workshop for high-school students at DevFest Windsor.

Get Tickets Register ($25, lunch included)

Greetings Parents,

We invite you to enroll your children in a one-day workshop specifically designed for high school students. Students will work in groups to assemble a car kit, program its controls, and develop collision avoidance logic using proximity sensors.

Mr. Ron McKenzie, a Vincent Massey High School Computer Science Teacher, is leading the workshop.

The workshop is part of a conference organized by the Google Developer Groups Windsor, and students will attend the conference keynote speech and closing remarks.

Students will work in groups, and the group will take the completed project with them.

  • Registration Fee: $25 (lunch included)
  • When: Saturday, November 8, 2025 — 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST
  • Location: Odette School of Business, 401 Sunset Avenue, Windsor, N9B 3P4
